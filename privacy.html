Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the BlockBlitz mobile game ("BlockBlitz", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number inside the app.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, scores, coins, stars, skins, streaks, missions and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive or store it on our own servers. Deleting the app deletes this data; your coin balance may be kept in your device's secure Keychain so it survives a reinstall.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

BlockBlitz shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Tracking permission: On iPhone, the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared and you will see non-personalized or less-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: Where required by law, a consent form from Google is shown before ads load. You can change your choices at any time from the app's Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • Remove Ads: Buying "Remove Ads" stops banner and between-level ads.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation of which item was bought so we can deliver it.

4. Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your scores are submitted to Game Center leaderboards and are visible to other Game Center players according to your Game Center settings. Apple's privacy policy applies to Game Center.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders (for example, when your daily reward is ready). These are created on your device; no notification data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

6. Children

BlockBlitz is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Because we don't keep personal data on our servers, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. Data collected by Google for advertising can be managed through your tracking and consent choices above and through Google's tools. If you live in the EEA, UK or another region with data-protection rights, you can contact us with any request and we will help.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email support@blockblitz.ma.
