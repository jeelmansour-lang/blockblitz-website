About

Made for the "one more round" feeling.

BlockBlitz is an independent block puzzle game for iPhone. We wanted the calm satisfaction of a classic block puzzle with more to chase: a campaign that keeps getting smarter, collectible skins that look good enough to want, and a mystery at the end of Endless that rewards real thinking.

🧩

Fair by design

A smart piece tray, a free lifeline when you're stuck, and stars that reward skill instead of luck.

🔮

Built for curiosity

Riddles, hidden seals and depths that change the game the further you go.

🛡️

Respectful

No account needed, progress stays on your device, ads are paced and removable.
