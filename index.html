How to play

Three steps. Endless strategy.

👆

Drag a piece

Pick one of the three pieces in your tray and drag it onto the 8×8 board. A preview shows exactly where it lands.

💥

Clear lines

Fill a full row or column to clear it. Clear several at once, or on back-to-back moves, for huge combos.

⭐

Beat the goal

Hit the level's score and line goals before you run out of space. Finish fast without continuing for 3 stars.

FAQ

Questions?

Is BlockBlitz free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play. It shows ads and offers optional purchases: Remove Ads, a one-time Starter Pack and coin packs.

What does Remove Ads do?

It removes banner and between-level ads forever, and makes every "watch a video" reward (continues, hints, rerolls, 2× coins) free. You can restore it on a new device from Settings → Restore purchases.

Can I play offline?

Yes, the game works without a connection. Ads, Game Center and purchases need internet.

Has anyone cracked the Endless Secret?

Not yet. The seals leaderboard in Game Center shows who has come closest. Five seals, in order, in one run.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. Your coin balance is also kept in the iPhone's secure Keychain so it survives a reinstall. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can always be restored.

How do I contact you?

Email support@blockblitz.ma or use Settings → Contact support in the app (it fills in your device details for us). See the Support page for quick fixes.
