GAME MODES

One game.
Two ways to play.

ENDLESS

Endless Mode

No clock. No level cap. Just you, the board, and the question of how long you can keep it going. Place pieces, clear lines, survive. Your high score is the only goal.

  • ♾️ Infinite gameplay — no lives, no timer
  • ⚡ Combo multipliers escalate without limit
  • 🏆 Score tracked on global leaderboard
  • 📈 Personal best displayed in real-time
LEVEL

Level Mode

Structured challenge with escalating difficulty. Each level has a target score and a piece limit. Finish efficiently to earn 3 stars. 150+ levels to conquer.

  • ⭐ Earn 1–3 stars per level
  • 🎯 Target score and line objectives
  • 📍 150+ levels unlocking progressively
  • 👑 Highest level tracked on your profile
